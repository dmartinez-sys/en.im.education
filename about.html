What makes IM different

Practice first. Our faculty are active professionals; our syllabi are updated continuously to match how companies actually work — including the real, day-to-day application of artificial intelligence across marketing, communication and business.

University partnerships. Our programmes are backed by partner universities including Universidad Tecnológica Atlántico Mediterráneo (UTAMED) and Universidad Abat Oliba CEU, combining university-recognised qualifications with IM's own Professional Mastery credentials.

Employability. Internship agreements, a dedicated job board and career support connect our students directly with the Spanish and European digital market.

Global community. Students from Europe and Latin America study with us on campus in Barcelona and Madrid, live online on a Spanish schedule, or fully online from anywhere in the world.

Recognition

IM holds QS Stars ratings and industry awards for digital education, and has been part of the Spanish digital-education landscape for more than two decades — from the first digital marketing master's programmes in Spain to today's AI-integrated curricula.
