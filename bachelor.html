Bachelor in Digital Business, Marketing & Artificial Intelligence

Business strategy, digital marketing and applied AI in one three-year programme — because companies no longer work in silos, and neither should your education.

One programme, three dimensions: business, marketing and AI

Organisations no longer operate in watertight compartments. Marketing depends on analytics; analytics depends on solid business and data structures; automation depends on processes, tools and judgement — and artificial intelligence only creates value when it's integrated into acquisition, conversion, retention and efficiency goals. This Bachelor was built around that reality.

Over three years you'll develop applicable specialisation: you start by supporting campaigns, content and reporting, and quickly progress to interpreting metrics, spotting opportunities, optimising funnels, using automation tools and understanding how AI improves productivity — the exact progression companies expect from junior digital profiles today.

The programme combines format flexibility (on-campus in Barcelona or Madrid, or online) with a direct link between learning and employability: real projects, industry faculty and access to IM's internship and job board.

What you'll learn

  • Digital business: business models, e-commerce, data-driven decision making, project management and entrepreneurship.
  • Marketing: strategy, content, social media, paid media, SEO, analytics and customer experience.
  • Artificial intelligence: AI tools and agents applied to marketing, automation, analysis and productivity — integrated across the whole programme, not bolted on at the end.

Who is it for?

  • Students finishing secondary education who want a career-focused alternative to a traditional business or marketing degree.
  • International students looking to study in Spain (Barcelona or Madrid) with an internship pathway into the Spanish and European digital market.
  • Anyone who wants to build a professional profile where business, marketing and real AI application already go hand in hand.
