University Partnerships

Joining forces with prestigious universities to reach new levels of excellence.

Partnering with leading universities to reach excellence

Since 2008, IM Digital Business School has driven knowledge through education built on capability and excellence, so our students strengthen their digital profile and grow their careers with confidence. That's why we collaborate with world-class universities, aligned with our strategy, that raise quality and back our educational mission.

Barcelona · Universitat Abat Oliba CEU

Universitat Abat Oliba CEU

Under the motto "Turn your vocation into reality", Universitat Abat Oliba CEU is part of the CEU educational group and one of its three social-initiative, privately managed universities.

Its commitment to well-rounded education and to each student's professional future shows in the internationalisation of its studies — including bilingual degree options — and in cultural, educational and scientific projects that have earned it the "Solidarity University" distinction, alongside a mission built on personalised attention, employability and leadership.

Málaga · UTAMED

Universidad Tecnológica Atlántico Mediterráneo (UTAMED)

UTAMED was created to bring distinctive value to the university system, with an innovative, rigorous academic offering aligned with today's challenges and focused on the continuous improvement of knowledge.

It places its community at the centre and sees higher education as an engine of development — driving teaching and research connected to the real world, and fostering collaboration across cultures, disciplines and companies to generate innovation and new opportunities.

Madrid · TECH Universidad

TECH Universidad

TECH presents itself as the world's largest digital university, with more than 14,000 academic programmes available in 11 languages and an international faculty of over 6,000 professors. Its 100% online model and employability focus have positioned it as a global reference in digital higher education.

Recognised by the institution itself as "the world's best digital university" according to Forbes, its offering is built on flexibility, educational innovation and applied learning, and it stands out for its Relearning methodology and international presence across academic markets.

Lima, Peru · Centrum PUCP

Centrum PUCP

With more than 10,000 graduates in leadership positions worldwide, Centrum PUCP holds eight excellence distinctions and four international accreditations: AACSB International, EQUIS, AMBA and BGA.

Under the motto "Redefining good business", it is the leading business school in Peru and top 10 in Latin America, with a mission focused on shaping agents of change and promoting socially responsible companies, guided by social responsibility, continuous improvement, innovation and academic excellence.
